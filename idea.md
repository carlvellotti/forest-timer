# Forest Timer

## The one line

A focus timer that grows a tree in your forest every time you finish a session.

## Who it's for

Me, and people like me who lose focus and like seeing progress.

## The problem

I start a focus session and then quit halfway through. And nothing shows me if my focus is adding up to anything.

## What it does

1. Starts a 25-minute focus session. Just 25 minutes, to keep it simple.
2. Grows a tree in my forest every time I actually finish one.
3. If I give up, no tree. It keeps a record that I gave up, but doesn't put it in the forest. Closing the tab halfway counts as giving up.
4. Shows my forest, so I can see my focus adding up.

## Possible future scope

- A streak.
- Picking my own session length instead of 25 minutes.
- Different kinds of trees.
- Friends seeing each other's forests. (Needs accounts and shared data, so it's for later.)

## Why it fits

It runs entirely in the browser, on the device of whoever opens it: no accounts, no shared data and no secret keys. The forest and the record of given-up sessions are saved in the browser, so they're still there next visit.
