# Stats page: grilling notes (2026-10-02)

The feature: a stats page showing this week's finished sessions and my streak.
Diagram test: a new Stats screen reading the saved session records. Nothing new is saved, all in the browser, so it's a new feature (not molding, not a climb).

## Q1. What does "this week's finished sessions" show?

Recommended: a count for each day, Monday to Sunday, plus the week's total ("9 sessions this week" above a row of seven days).
Other options: just the total; a list of each session with date, time and length.

> yep, per day plus the total

## Q2. What is "this week"?

Recommended: the calendar week, Monday to Sunday, in your own time zone. On Wednesday, Monday to Wednesday are filled in and Thursday to Sunday are still empty; Monday starts fresh at zero.
Other option: the last 7 days, always ending today.

> yes, monday to sunday

## Q3. Which day does a session count on if it crosses midnight?

Recommended: the day it finished (start 11:50pm Tuesday, finish 12:15am Wednesday → Wednesday). That's when the tree grew.
Other option: the day it started.

> yep the day it finished

## Q4. What's your streak in the morning, before you've finished anything today?

Recommended: finished Mon, Tue, Wed; Thursday 9am with nothing yet → still 3. Today isn't over, so it isn't broken; it becomes 4 when you finish one today, and drops to 0 only once a whole day passes with nothing (Friday reads 0 until you finish one).
Other option: 0 until today's first finished session.

> yes 3, no scolding at breakfast

## Q5. Do given-up sessions appear anywhere on the stats page?

Recommended: not in this version. They don't count toward the week or the streak and don't show; they stay saved, and go back on possible future scope.
Other option: a quiet extra line, like "2 given up" under the week's total.

> leave them out for now

## Q6. How do you get to the stats page, and back?

Recommended: a quiet "Stats" link (muted, underlined, like "Give up") at the top-right corner, opening the stats page at its own address, /stats, so it can be bookmarked. The stats page has a quiet "Back to forest" link.
Other options: no separate page (week and streak on the main screen); the same link with no /stats address.

> yes the quiet link and /stats. what happens if i tap it in the middle of a session though?

## Q7. What about the Stats link during a session?

Recommended: the Stats link only shows when Ready, and is hidden while Running, so it can't pull you away mid-session. Edge case: typing /stats mid-session is a page load, so the reload rule applies and the session carries on; the stats page shows "A session is running, 12:34 left" with a link back to the timer, and if it finishes there it still counts (tree, chime, this week's numbers).
Other options: show the link while running and keep the session going in the background; show it but ask first, like Give up.

> hide it while running, makes sense

## Q8. How does each day in the week look?

Recommended: plain numbers. Seven short day names (Mon … Sun), each with its count under it in muted ink (DESIGN.md's color for counts and dates); today marked in the main text color; days with nothing show "0"; days still to come are blank.
Example: "9 sessions this week" above Mon 4, Tue 3, Wed 2, Thu 0, Fri–Sun blank.
Other option: small trees instead of numbers, one per finished session under each day.

> plain numbers

## Q9. How does the streak read, including at zero?

Recommended: one line above the week, "3-day streak" ("1-day streak" for one), at the timer's size, as the page's one big thing. At zero: "No streak yet. Finish a session to start one." in body text and ink, like the first-visit line. With no sessions at all, the week shows "0 sessions this week" with zeros up to today.
Other options: the week big and the streak smaller; both the same size.

> yep streak big, week under it

Possible future scope noted while grilling: given-up sessions on the stats page.
No decision records: everything here is worked out from the saved session records, nothing new is saved, so it's all easy to change later.
