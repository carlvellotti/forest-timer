- [x] Slice 2: Finish a session and a tree grows in your forest, still there after a refresh
  Needs: slice 1
  Adds: Browser storage (session records), the forest on the Screen
  Connects: Session keeper → storage, storage → Screen ("finished sessions become trees")
  Checks:
  - First visit: "Finish a session to grow your first tree." in muted grey
  - Rule 2: start at 9:00; at 9:25 one ringed tree sits top-left, the timer reads 25:00, and the first-visit line is gone
  - Press Start at 9:30, and the ring is gone
  - Refresh: the tree is still there, without the ring
  - A finished session record is saved: started 9:00, ended 9:25, finished
  - Rule 9: start at 9:00, the clock jumps to 9:40, and the session has finished with a tree
  Filled in:
  - Each tree sits in a 24px square, with room inside for the ring (added to DESIGN.md and the theme; DESIGN.md had no tree size)
  - The tree is a two-tier pine drawn from DESIGN.md's tree colors
  - The forest sits at the bottom of the screen, under the line, and the timer stays centered in the space above
  - The first-visit line uses the small text size (14px)
  - A finished session's "ended" time is 25 minutes after Start, even if it was noticed later (after sleep)
  - Saved data the app can't read is kept aside under its own name rather than wiped (rule 10)
  - If saving fails (storage full or blocked), the screen still goes back to ready; that session's tree just isn't saved

- [x] Slice 5: One soft chime when a session finishes
  Needs: slice 2
  Adds: Chime
  Connects: Session keeper → Chime ("finished")
  Checks:
  - Finishing plays the chime once; giving up plays nothing
  - It plays while the page is in a background tab
  - After a locked phone (page frozen past 9:25) comes back, it plays at the same moment the tree appears (simulated with a frozen page)
  - You hear the note and approve it (a check only you can do)
  Filled in:
  - The note: one sine E5, rising over 20ms and fading out over about 2½ seconds (heard and approved 2026-10-02, "fine for now")
  - Sound is switched on when you press Start, because browsers won't play sound before you've pressed something
  - Each session also sets one timer for its exact end, so a background tab (where Chrome slows the once-a-second tick) still finishes and chimes on time
  - With an iPhone's silent switch on, there's no chime: silent means silent (decided 2026-10-02)
  - Couldn't check: a real iPhone after a lock, where iOS may not wake the sound until you tap

- [x] Slice 6: A reload mid-session carries on; a reload after the 25 minutes counts as finished
  Needs: slices 2 and 5
  Connects: storage → Session keeper ("on opening: was a session running?")
  Checks:
  - Rule 4: start at 9:00, reload at 9:10, and the timer shows 15:00
  - Rule 5: start at 9:00, reload at 9:40: you get the tree, with its ring, and no chime (changed 2026-10-02: browsers won't play sound after a reload until you press something; the ring is enough)
  Filled in:
  - The moment Start was pressed is saved as soon as you press it, and cleared when the session ends
  - A tab the browser put away to save memory and brought back counts as a reload too
  - Opening the app fresh mid-session (after a close or a crash) shows ready and forgets the session, so a later reload can't bring it back; recording it as a give-up is slice 7
  - A saved start time in the future (the clock was moved back) is forgotten, and the timer never shows more than 25:00
  - If a late reload's tree can't be saved, it gets no ring, and the next reload tries again
  - Open: after a reload mid-session, the session finishes without the chime unless sound gets switched back on (see the handback)
