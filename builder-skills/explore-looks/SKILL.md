Then keep what I picked and change one thing at a time (colors, fonts, spacing, corners, motion, icons), for as many rounds as I want.
Don't fall back on the usual AI look: no cream backgrounds, purple gradients or grids of identical cards, unless my mood board asks for them.
The sketches are just for choosing. Don't save them in my project.
